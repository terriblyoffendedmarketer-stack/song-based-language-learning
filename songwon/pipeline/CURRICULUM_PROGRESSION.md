# Curriculum Progression — Grammar-First, Songs Second

Status: DRAFT — for review before implementation

## The Problem

Current approach: each song gets its own lesson, grammar/vocab is whatever that song happens to contain. This means a Level 1 beginner might learn -게 되다 (TOPIK 3) in their first lesson because 10cm uses it, while never learning -고 싶다 (TOPIK 1) because no Level 1 song was assigned to it.

## The Fix

Define the grammar + vocab progression first (following proven Korean language teaching sequences), then find song lines that demonstrate each concept. A single lesson might pull lines from multiple songs.

## Proposed Lesson Sequence

Based on King Sejong Institute / TOPIK progression, adapted for our corpus of 72 songs (250 available).

---

### Unit 1: The Basics — Connecting & Wanting

**Lesson 1: And, And Then (-고)**
- Grammar: -고 (and / and then)
- Vocab: 꿈 (dream), 사랑 (love), 밤 (night), 웃다 (to smile)
- Song sources: 10cm - Phonecert, Sogyumo Acacia Band - Butterfly
- Concept: Connecting two actions or states. The simplest way to link ideas.

**Lesson 2: I Want To (-고 싶다)**
- Grammar: -고 싶다 (want to do)
- Vocab: 찾다 (to find), 사람 (person), 길 (road/way), 말하다 (to say)
- Song sources: BTS - Save Me, Gaho - 시작, DAY6 - Shoot Me, IU - Through the Night
- Concept: Expressing desires. One of the first things you need in any language.

**Lesson 3: Right Now (-고 있다)**
- Grammar: -고 있다 (progressive / ongoing state)
- Vocab: 기다리다 (to wait), 떠나다 (to leave), 지키다 (to protect)
- Song sources: Punch - Done For Me, Punch - Stay With Me, Red Velvet - Psycho
- Concept: Describing what's happening right now. "I'm waiting" vs "I wait."

**Lesson 4: If... (-（으)면)**
- Grammar: -(으)면 (if / when)
- Vocab: 괜찮다 (to be okay), 하늘 (sky), 바람 (wind), 시간 (time)
- Song sources: IVE - XOXZ, MOMOLAND - BBoom BBoom, Punch - Stay With Me
- Concept: Conditional statements. "If you smile, I'm happy."

---

### Unit 2: Contrast & Negation

**Lesson 5: But (-지만)**
- Grammar: -지만 (but, although)
- Vocab: 아프다 (to hurt), 세상 (world), 기억 (memory), 마음 (heart)
- Song sources: DAY6 - You Were Beautiful, 10cm - Stalker, MAMAMOO - Mr-Ambiguous
- Concept: Expressing contrast. "I want to forget, but I can't."

**Lesson 6: I Can't (-지 못하다)**
- Grammar: -지 못하다 (cannot do)
- Vocab: 멈추다 (to stop), 잊다 (to forget), 놓다 (to let go)
- Song sources: IU - Good Day, LUCY - I Got U, Ha Hyun Sang - Lighthouse
- Concept: Inability. "I can't stop thinking about you."

**Lesson 7: Don't / Not (-지 않다)**
- Grammar: -지 않다 (not, negation)
- Vocab: 변하다 (to change), 끝 (end), 다시 (again)
- Song sources: TWICE - Likey, Lim Jae Beum - 낙인, Shin In Ryu - Undecided
- Concept: Standard negation. "It doesn't change."

**Lesson 8: Because (-아/어서)**
- Grammar: -아/어서 (because / and then)
- Vocab: 보다 (to see), 듣다 (to hear), 느끼다 (to feel)
- Song sources: Punch - Stay With Me, YOUNGJOO - Smells
- Concept: Giving reasons. "Because I love you, I'll stay."

---

### Unit 3: Requests, Opinions, Context

**Lesson 9: Please Do It For Me (-아/어 줘)**
- Grammar: -아/어 줘 (please do ~ for me)
- Vocab: 떠나다 (to leave), 잡다 (to hold/grab), 안다 (to hug)
- Song sources: SHAUN - Way Back Home, JO YURI - GLASSY, BEN - Just Like a Dream
- Concept: Making requests. Essential for real conversation.

**Lesson 10: I Think... (-(으)ㄹ 것 같다)**
- Grammar: -(으)ㄹ 것 같다 (it seems like / I think)
- Vocab: 빛나다 (to shine), 흐르다 (to flow), 울다 (to cry)
- Song sources: IU - Through the Night, BTS - Life Goes On, MOMOLAND - BBoom BBoom
- Concept: Expressing opinions softly. Koreans use this constantly.

**Lesson 11: You Know... (-잖아)**
- Grammar: -잖아 (you know / isn't it / reminding)
- Vocab: 알다 (to know), 보이다 (to be seen), 남다 (to remain)
- Song sources: BOL4 - Love story, BTS - Save Me, LUCY - I Got U, 10cm - Phonecert
- Concept: Reminding someone of something they already know. Very casual/intimate.

**Lesson 12: Background Info (-는데)**
- Grammar: -는데 (background info / mild contrast)
- Vocab: 걷다 (to walk), 비 (rain), 바라보다 (to gaze at)
- Song sources: BOL4 - Some, BEN - Just Like a Dream, Lundi Blues - Fake
- Concept: Setting the scene before your main point. Extremely common in spoken Korean.

---

### Unit 4: Emotions & Changes

**Lesson 13: Even Though (-아/어도)**
- Grammar: -아/어도 (even though, even if)
- Vocab: 사라지다 (to disappear), 곁 (side), 잊다 (to forget)
- Song sources: BOL4 - Some, BTS - FAKE LOVE, Gaho - 시작, DAVICHI - 그대니까요
- Concept: Concession. "Even if you leave, I'll be okay." One of the most common patterns in Korean songs.

**Lesson 14: Becoming (-아/어지다)**
- Grammar: -아/어지다 (to become a state)
- Vocab: 멀어지다 (to grow distant), 행복하다 (to be happy), 슬프다 (to be sad)
- Song sources: Multiple — 10 songs use this pattern
- Concept: Describing changes of state. "It's getting colder." "I'm becoming lonely."

**Lesson 15: Like, As If (-처럼)**
- Grammar: -처럼 (like, as)
- Vocab: 별 (star), 꽃 (flower), 계절 (season)
- Song sources: BEN - Just Like a Dream, Gaho - 시작, Girls' Generation - Lion Heart
- Concept: Comparisons. "Like a dream." "Like a star."

**Lesson 16: Ending Up (-게 되다)**
- Grammar: -게 되다 (to end up ~ing)
- Vocab: 목소리 (voice), 들리다 (to be heard), 좋아하다 (to like)
- Song sources: 10cm - My Eyes, BTS - FAKE LOVE, MOMOLAND - BBoom BBoom
- Concept: Things that happen gradually or beyond your control. "I ended up loving you."

---

### Unit 5: Intensity & Regret

**Lesson 17: Completely Done (-아/어 버리다)**
- Grammar: -아/어 버리다 (to do completely / with finality or regret)
- Vocab: 버리다 (to throw away), 지우다 (to erase), 떠나다 (to leave)
- Song sources: 7 songs including BTS - FAKE LOVE, 10cm - My Eyes
- Concept: Something done completely, often with a sense of "dammit, it happened." "I fell for you completely."

**Lesson 18: Before Doing (-기 전에)**
- Grammar: -기 전에 (before doing)
- Vocab: 시작하다 (to start), 끝나다 (to end), 닫다 (to close)
- Song sources: 4 songs
- Concept: Time sequencing. "Before you leave."

**Lesson 19: Even While (-다가도)**
- Grammar: -다가도 (even while in the middle of doing)
- Vocab: 생각하다 (to think), 혼자 (alone), 그리다 (to miss/draw)
- Song sources: 4 songs
- Concept: Contradictory simultaneous states. "Even while laughing, I cry."

**Lesson 20: The Past (-던)**
- Grammar: -던 (past retrospective modifier)
- Vocab: 추억 (memory/nostalgia), 흔적 (trace), 향기 (scent)
- Song sources: 9 songs — most used TOPIK 4 pattern
- Concept: Describing things as they were in the past. "The street we used to walk."

## How This Differs From Current Curriculum

| Current (song-first) | Proposed (grammar-first) |
|---|---|
| Each song = one lesson | Each grammar point = one lesson |
| Grammar is whatever the song has | Grammar follows TOPIK progression |
| Level 1 songs might teach TOPIK 3 grammar | TOPIK 1 grammar comes first, always |
| One song per lesson | Multiple songs per lesson (best lines from each) |
| 72 lessons (one per song) | ~20 core lessons initially, expandable |
| Random vocab | Vocab follows frequency + level |

## Song Coverage

With 20 lessons pulling from 72 songs, each lesson uses 2-4 songs for examples. Songs with rich grammar (BTS - FAKE LOVE, Gaho - 시작, Punch - Stay With Me) appear in multiple lessons. Songs with less grammar get featured when their vocab or theme fits.

Not every song needs its own lesson. Some songs are better as supplementary material (extra lyrics practice, sing-along sessions, review material).

## Open Questions

1. **How many lessons per unit?** Currently 4 each, could be 3-5.
2. **Should each lesson still be tied to ONE primary song for the intro/sing-along?** Even if vocab comes from multiple songs?
3. **Vocab per lesson: 4 words or 3?** More words = more screens = longer session. For ADHD, shorter might be better.
4. **When do we introduce Hangul reading?** Or do we assume the learner can already read it?
5. **Review/revision lessons between units?** Like Duolingo's "checkpoint" lessons.
6. **The curriculum ordering idea — should we also research what LingoDeer/Duolingo specifically teach in their first 20 Korean lessons?** That would give us a concrete benchmark.
